### Welcome!

The BiomedLab is proud to be the first research laboratory dedicated to Biomedical Engineering in Guatemala. This pioneering laboratory’s core mission is to research, design, and develop advanced technologies that provide effective solutions to current challenges in the healthcare sector, both in Guatemala and across the region.

### About us

BiomedLab works to strengthen technological capabilities and drive the development of tools tailored to the specific needs of our population. The laboratory is also committed to cultivating local talent and fostering collaboration at both national and international levels among academics, healthcare professionals, and technology experts. Our research areas include medical image analysis, biomedical signal processing, medical instrumentation & assisted robotic, and the application of artificial intelligence to healthcare challenges.
