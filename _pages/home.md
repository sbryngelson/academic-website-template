### Welcome!

The BiomedLab is proud to be the first research laboratory dedicated to Biomedical Engineering in Guatemala. This pioneering laboratory’s core mission is to research, design, and develop advanced technologies that provide effective solutions to current challenges in the healthcare sector, both in Guatemala and across the region.

### About us

BiomedLab works to strengthen technological capabilities and promote the creation of new tools that meet the specific needs of our population. It is also committed to developing local talent and encouraging collaboration at both the national and international level among academics, healthcare professionals, and technology experts. With this vision, the laboratory not only provides solutions to current challenges but also prepares for those to come, positioning itself as a leader in biomedical engineering research and development in the region.
