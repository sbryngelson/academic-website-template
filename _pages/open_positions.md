## Join the Quantum Devices & Simulation Group

We’re building quantum hardware and algorithms that push the state of the art.  
If you’re excited about **quantum devices**, **quantum simulation**, or **computational physics/chemistry**, we’d love to hear from you.



### How to apply (all roles)
Please send a **single PDF** to <a href="mailto:matti.silveri@univ.oulu.fi">matti.silveri@univ.oulu.fi</a> with:
1. **Cover letter** (1 page) – interests & fit; preferred track (Devices / Simulation)
2. **CV** – include publications / projects & links (GitHub/Scholar)
3. **Transcript(s)**
4. **Referees** – names & emails (no letters needed initially)

We value a diverse, inclusive team. If you’re unsure you meet every requirement, we still encourage you to apply.

---

### Why Oulu & our group?
- Excellent Theoretical and Numarical work i transmon qubits(Devices track)
- Active collaborations in quantum algorithms and quantum chemistry (Simulation track)
- Support for conference travel and research visits
- A friendly, international team culture 🌟
