### <a id="quantum-devices"></a>Quantum Devices

This section brings together experimentalists, engineers, and theorists to advance the development of quantum hardware. By combining expertise in device fabrication, measurement techniques, and theoretical modeling, we aim to push the boundaries of quantum technology and enable new applications.

### <a id="quantum-simulation"></a>Quantum Simulation

Focusing on quantum algorithms and simulation, this section unites computational scientists, algorithm developers, and domain experts. The team works on designing efficient quantum algorithms, developing novel simulation techniques, and applying these methods to solve complex problems in physics, chemistry, and materials science




<div class="jumbotron">
<div class="col-md-12 col-sm-12">
<h4> Research</h4>

Developing optimal ansatzes is essential for advancing VQAs and quantum
chemistry simulations. Addressing scalability and barren plateaus, we aim
to create novel ansatzes and algorithms to improve these simulations. VQAs
have been extensively studied in the literature, resulting in many versions.
However, identifying the optimal approach and designing problem-specific
ansatzes remain unresolved challenges.
Understanding the dynamics of Hamiltonians within chemical accuracy
not only advances quantum chemistry simulations but also has the potential
to significantly impact science and society.
Therefore, the ultimate goal of this project is to address challenges such as
Barren Plateaus and optimal ansatz design. In our project, we will specially
focus on quantum computing use-cases relevant to H2Future research themes:
first, transition metal compounds and oxides and, later, simplified models for
photo-catalysis.
